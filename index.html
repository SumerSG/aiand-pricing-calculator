How cost is calculated

monthly_cost = calls_per_month × (input_tokens / 1M × input_price + output_tokens / 1M × output_price)

Prices are USD per 1M tokens, from the ai& pricing docs. ai& bills against a prepaid credit balance, deducting atomically when each request completes. Prompt caching (automatic on prompts ≥1,024 tokens) can reduce input costs further.

Quality and popularity are the Theozard quality score and OpenRouter popularity rank from the Theozard LLM Leaderboard — — means the model isn't listed there.
