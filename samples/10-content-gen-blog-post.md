# 5 Ways AI Cuts Support Costs Without Cutting Corners

*Generated sample — Content Gen preset (1.5K input / 3K output tokens).*

Your support queue isn't shrinking. Headcount can't scale linearly with ticket
volume — and it doesn't have to. Here are five practical ways teams use AI to
bend the cost curve.

## 1. Deflect the repetitive 42%
How-to questions make up nearly half of inbound volume. An AI agent trained on
your help center closes these instantly, at pennies per resolution.

## 2. Draft, don't write
For tickets that need a human, AI-drafted replies with full context cut
handle time by a third.

## 3. Triage automatically
Correct routing on first touch eliminates the multi-transfer cost spiral.

## 4. Let customers self-serve at 2 a.m.
Async, instant answers — no night shift required.

## 5. Turn transcripts into insight
Every conversation becomes structured data: churn signals, product gaps,
documentation fixes.

*The takeaway: automation isn't about replacing agents — it's about reserving
them for conversations that matter.*
